Privacy policy

BattleCore

Effective date: 19 September 2026
iOS bundle ID: com.battlecore.game

Short version: BattleCore for iPhone and iPad is a paid, offline single-player game. It has no ads, in-app purchases, subscriptions, analytics, or account sign-in.

Information collection

BattleCore does not ask Ojaskara Studio for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or account details. The iOS app does not transmit gameplay or personal data to Ojaskara Studio or advertising and analytics partners. Apple processes the App Store purchase under its own terms and privacy policy; BattleCore does not receive payment details.

Information stored on your device

Campaign progress, unlocked ships and power cores, cosmetic selections, and settings are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to Ojaskara Studio. You can remove local data by uninstalling the game. iOS backup or restore may retain or restore local app data according to your device and Apple account settings.

Permissions and connectivity

The campaign is playable offline and BattleCore does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, media library, or precise location. The game does not require an internet connection to play and does not request tracking permission.

Sharing and sale

Ojaskara Studio does not collect or sell personal information through the iOS app. Apple handles the App Store purchase under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

BattleCore is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Security and retention

BattleCore does not operate its own player-data server. Local game data remains on your device until you uninstall the game, subject to iOS backup and restore behavior. Apple handles App Store transaction records under its own policies.

Changes to this policy

Ojaskara Studio may update this policy when the game or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be revised before material changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about BattleCore or this policy can be sent to ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com or through ojaskarastudio.com.
