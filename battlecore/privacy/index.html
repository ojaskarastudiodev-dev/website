Privacy policy

BattleCore

Effective date: 31 August 2026
Android package: com.battlecore.game

Short version: BattleCore is an offline single-player game. It has no accounts, advertising, purchases, analytics, or developer-operated backend, and Ojaskara Studio does not collect or share personal data through the game.

Information collection

BattleCore does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, payment information, precise location, advertising identifiers, or other personal information. The game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or account services.

Information stored on your device

Campaign progress, unlocked ships and power cores, cosmetic selections, and game settings are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide game features and is not transmitted to Ojaskara Studio. You can remove it by clearing BattleCore's app storage or uninstalling the game. Android backup or restore features may retain or restore local app data according to your device and Google account settings.

Permissions and connectivity

BattleCore is playable offline and does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts, media library, or precise location. It uses ordinary device capabilities needed to display the game, play audio, accept touch input, and provide optional on-device vibration.

Sharing and sale

Ojaskara Studio does not sell personal information and does not share personal information collected through BattleCore because the game does not collect it.

Children

BattleCore is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Security and retention

BattleCore does not operate a server that stores player information. Local game data remains on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the game, subject to Android backup and restore behavior.

Changes to this policy

Ojaskara Studio may update this policy when the game or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will be revised before material changes take effect.

Contact

Questions about BattleCore or this policy can be sent to ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com or through ojaskarastudio.com.
