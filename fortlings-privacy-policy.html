Fortlings Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 30, 2026

Short version: Fortlings is a single-player game with no player account. It uses Google services for ads, consent, analytics, crash reporting, and the optional Remove Ads purchase. Ojaskara Studio does not sell personal data.

Fortlings ("the Game") is developed and published by Ojaskara Studio. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Fortlings does not require a player account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment-card details. Google Mobile Ads, the User Messaging Platform, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play Billing may process app interactions, advertising or app-instance identifiers, approximate location inferred from network information, diagnostics, crash information, device and app metadata, consent choices, and purchase status as applicable to each service and your device or consent settings.

2. Local Game Data

Campaign progress, stage ratings, cosmetic selections, tutorial completion, settings, advertising cadence, and a cached Remove Ads entitlement are stored locally on your device. You can remove this data by using the in-game campaign reset where available, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the Game. Android backup or restore features may retain or restore local app data according to your device and Google account settings.

3. Permissions and Device Features

Fortlings uses normal device features needed to present gameplay, including audio, touch input, screen display, and optional device vibration for haptic feedback. Haptic feedback is processed on the device. The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or media library.

4. Advertising and Purchases

Fortlings may show optional rewarded ads and occasional interstitial ads supplied through Google Mobile Ads. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform asks for or records privacy choices before advertising is requested, and the Settings screen provides a privacy-options entry point when required. Fortlings offers a one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads purchase through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details; Fortlings receives product, purchase-state, and token information needed to grant, acknowledge, restore, refund, or revoke the entitlement. There are no subscriptions or paid gameplay currencies.

5. Analytics and Crash Reporting

Firebase Analytics records coarse gameplay and screen events, build information, and progression bands so Ojaskara Studio can understand usage and improve the Game. No developer-defined player name, email address, or precise location is sent. Firebase Crashlytics processes crash stack traces, relevant application state, device metadata, installation identifiers, and limited gameplay context attached to a crash so stability problems can be diagnosed.

6. Children's Privacy

Ojaskara Studio does not knowingly request names, contact details, precise location, or payment information from children through the Game. Google services may apply age-appropriate or child-directed handling based on the app's Play configuration, device/account signals, and applicable law. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the address below.

7. Service Providers, Sharing, and Retention

Google LLC provides advertising, consent, analytics, crash reporting, and billing services and processes information under Google's privacy policy and applicable service terms. Ojaskara Studio does not sell personal data. Locally stored game data remains until it is reset, cleared, restored by Android backup, or removed as described above. Retention of information processed by Google is controlled by the applicable Google service settings and policies.

8. Your Choices

Where available, use Privacy Options in Settings to review advertising consent choices. You may decline a rewarded ad and continue using normal Retry or Levels controls. You may restore an existing Remove Ads purchase through Settings. Device advertising and privacy controls are also available in Android settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Fortlings changes or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Material changes will be published before the affected game update.
