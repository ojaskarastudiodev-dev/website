Fortlings Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 24, 2026

Short version: Fortlings is an offline, single-player game. The current version does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Fortlings ("the Game") is developed and published by Ojaskara Studio. This policy explains how information is handled when you play the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Fortlings does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or other personal information. The current version does not include analytics, advertising, attribution, or crash-reporting SDKs and does not transmit gameplay information to Ojaskara Studio.

2. Local Game Data

Campaign progress, stage ratings, tutorial completion, and game settings are stored locally on your device. You can remove this data by using the in-game campaign reset where available, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the Game. Android backup or restore features may retain or restore local app data according to your device and Google account settings.

3. Permissions and Device Features

Fortlings uses normal device features needed to present gameplay, including audio, touch input, screen display, and optional device vibration for haptic feedback. Haptic feedback is processed on the device. The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or media library.

4. Advertising and Purchases

The current closed-testing version contains no advertisements, in-app purchases, subscriptions, or rewarded-ad flows. If a future version introduces any of these features or related third-party services, this policy and the Google Play Data safety disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Fortlings does not collect personal information, Ojaskara Studio does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the Game. Parents or guardians with questions may contact us using the address below.

6. Data Sharing and Retention

Ojaskara Studio does not receive or share personal data from the current version of Fortlings. Locally stored game data remains on your device until it is reset, cleared, or removed as described above.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when Fortlings changes or when legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version. Material changes will be published before the affected game update.
