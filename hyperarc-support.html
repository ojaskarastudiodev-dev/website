HyperArc support

Help with racing, flight and space combat.

Get started

Open Campaign, choose Race or Hunt, select an arena and start Level 1. Complete levels to unlock later challenges. Quick Play selects an available challenge. The Garage previews ships; unlocks are earned by completing unique levels.

Controls and abilities

Circuit supports touch and optional tilt steering. SkyArc and Hunt use a virtual joystick. The in-game Controls screen explains steering, ARC Drive, firing preferences and joystick side. Combat includes guns and homing missiles. Shield is available once per stage; Wingmen are available once per combat stage from stage 2.

Progress and troubleshooting

Progress is stored locally. Reinstalling or deleting app data can remove progress. There is no cross-device cloud save. For audio issues, check Settings and your device volume. Pause before switching away from a challenge; return and resume when ready.

Contact us

Email ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com with your device model, OS version, app version, arena/level and steps to reproduce the issue. Avoid including passwords or sensitive personal information.

Privacy and purchase

No player account, advertisements or in-app purchases are required. Gameplay works offline. See the HyperArc privacy policy. The iOS release awaits App Store approval; Android is in closed testing.
