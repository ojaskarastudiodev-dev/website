HyperArc

Race the circuit. Hunt through the asteroids. Make the ship yours.

A single-player, offline sci-fi racing and spacecraft-combat game with four arenas and 20 levels.

Four ways to fly

Race rival ships in Circuit, thread flight rings in SkyArc, and face spacecraft battles in BlackStar and Nereid. Complete levels to unlock more challenges and cosmetic ship choices.

Choose from five ships and three finishes. In combat, use guns, homing missiles, a temporary shield, and two temporary Wingmen from combat stage 2.

Made for offline play

One upfront purchase. No advertisements, accounts, subscriptions, or in-app purchases. Progress and settings stay on your device; cross-device cloud saves are not included.

Availability

The iPhone and iPad release is awaiting App Store approval. The Android version is currently in closed testing and is not publicly released.
