Privacy policy

MySigns

Effective date: 19 August 2026
App: MySigns
Android package: com.ojaskara.mysigns

Ojaskara Studio built MySigns as an adult lifestyle and entertainment app. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored, and the choices available to you.

Information stored on your device

MySigns stores your first name, date of birth, optional birth time, notification preference, generated readings, streak, and rewarded-reading unlock state locally on your device. This information is used to personalize the experience. Ojaskara Studio does not operate an account system or backend profile database, and does not transmit your profile or reading text to its servers.

Analytics

MySigns uses Firebase Analytics to understand general app use and improve the experience. Firebase may process app interactions, installation or device identifiers, and related device information. Custom analytics events use semantic values such as zodiac sign, score band, or whether an optional birth time exists; they do not include your name, date of birth, exact birth time, persisted profile, or reading text.

Crash reports and diagnostics

MySigns uses Firebase Crashlytics to identify stability problems. Crashlytics may process stack traces, relevant application state, diagnostics, device metadata, and a Crashlytics installation UUID. This helps Ojaskara Studio investigate crashes and improve reliability.

Rewarded advertising

MySigns uses Google AdMob to offer optional rewarded ads. Google Mobile Ads may process IP-derived approximate location, advertising or app-set identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and information used for fraud prevention and security. MySigns initially requests non-personalized rewarded ads. A reading unlock is granted only after the ad provider reports that the reward was earned.

Notifications

If you choose to enable reminders, MySigns requests notification permission and schedules notifications locally on your device. You can change this choice in MySigns Settings or Android system settings.

Sharing, sale, and service providers

Ojaskara Studio does not sell personal information. Firebase and Google Mobile Ads process the limited data described above as service providers under their own terms and privacy policies. Learn more in the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

Security and retention

Applicable provider traffic is encrypted in transit. Locally stored data remains until you clear MySigns app data or uninstall the app. Analytics, crash, and advertising data are retained according to the applicable Google and Firebase retention policies.

Your choices

  • Do not provide optional birth time information.
  • Enable or disable notifications at any time.
  • Use the ad privacy choices entry in Settings when privacy options are required.
  • Clear local information through Android storage controls or uninstall MySigns.

Children

MySigns is intended only for people aged 18 and over and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

Ojaskara Studio may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com.
