Privacy Policy

SolveBox — Last updated: August 8, 2026

SolveBox ("the App") is developed by Ojaskara Studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

SolveBox does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, or other direct contact information. Game progress and puzzle results are stored locally on your device.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect data automatically:

  • Google AdMob – used to display rewarded ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, IP address, and usage data to serve personalised or non-personalised ads in accordance with Google's privacy policies.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase – used to understand app usage and improve the puzzles. It may collect app interactions (such as level starts, completions, hint usage, language choice, and ad-flow events), device and app information, approximate location derived from IP address, and identifiers provided by the SDK. We do not send puzzle progress saved on your device, microphone recordings, or your name or email address to Analytics.
  • Firebase Crashlytics – used to diagnose crashes and technical problems. It may collect crash logs, diagnostics, app state at the time of a problem, device and operating-system information, and installation identifiers.

2. How Data Is Used

We use AdMob data to deliver and measure advertisements, Analytics data to understand engagement and improve the game, and Crashlytics data to maintain stability and investigate errors. We do not sell your personal information. Google processes information for these services under its own terms and privacy policies.

3. Sensors & Device Permissions

Certain puzzle challenges use on-device sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope, microphone, step counter). Sensor data is processed entirely on-device in real-time and is never stored or transmitted.

The microphone permission is requested only for sound-based challenges. No audio is recorded or stored.

4. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

5. Data Retention

Game progress stored locally on your device can be cleared by resetting progress in the App or uninstalling it. Information processed by Google is retained in accordance with the retention settings and policies for AdMob and Firebase. Aggregated or de-identified reports may be retained for product analysis.

6. Your Choices

You can avoid rewarded-ad requests by not choosing an ad-supported hint or skip. You can clear local game progress from the App. You may also reset your device's advertising identifier or manage ad personalisation through your Google settings. To request information or deletion relating to data under our control, contact us using the address below.

7. Website Feedback

When you voluntarily submit the feedback form on our website, we receive the name, email address, topic, and message you provide. This information is used only to read and respond to your feedback.

Form submissions are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service. Do not include passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in your message.

8. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may display ads containing links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and Security information, and AdMob policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
