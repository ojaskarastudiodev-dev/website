RiftHook Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026. Published by Akshaya Sripathi under the name Ojaskara Studio.

Offline gameplay and local data

RiftHook works offline without a player account. It does not transmit gameplay or personal information to our servers. Progress, settings, best times, unlocks and replay ghosts are saved locally on your device. This release has no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not access contacts, location, camera or microphone. Optional vibration provides gameplay feedback.

Purchases and platform services

RiftHook is a one-time paid download without ads or in-app purchases. Apple or Google processes purchases under its own privacy policy; we do not receive payment-card details. Stores may provide sales reports and platform diagnostics. TestFlight may provide Apple-collected testing, crash and feedback information. These services are separate from the offline game.

Support

Emailing ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com shares your email address and any information you choose to send with us and our email provider. We use it to respond and troubleshoot. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Correspondence is retained only as needed to resolve requests, maintain necessary records or meet legal obligations. Contact us to request access or deletion.

Website and links

Privacy and support links open your browser and require internet access. GitHub Pages hosts these pages and may process technical request information such as IP addresses to provide and secure hosting under its privacy statement. These RiftHook pages add no advertising cookies or analytics scripts. Email and app-store services have their own privacy policies.

Sharing and your choices

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Support information may be disclosed when legally required. Email and hosting providers may process information internationally under their own safeguards. Local game data stays on your device. Device backups, if enabled, are controlled by your platform. Clearing app storage or deleting the app and its data removes local progress; restoring backups may restore it. We do not operate cloud saves.

Children

The game does not ask players to submit personal information. If a child sends personal information to support, contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and effective date when practices change. Privacy questions: ojaskarastudio.dev@gmail.com.
