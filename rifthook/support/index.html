RiftHook support

Controls

Use your movement thumb to steer, and move vertically to adjust rope length. Hold free gameplay space to grapple the highlighted reachable hook; release to detach. When available, hold the jetpack button to fly. Settings offers joystick or direction-pad controls, left/right layouts, audio and haptics.

Progress and troubleshooting

Progress is stored locally. Deleting app data may erase it. There is no account or cloud sync. Restart the game if controls become unresponsive. For sound issues, check game settings and device volume.
